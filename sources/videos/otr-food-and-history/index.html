OTR Food & History

English-language YouTube videos about the history of Thai dishes

Sources

“OTR Food & History” is a YouTube channel that was started in August 2022. The American Adam Gottschalk and his small team tell stories about food in Thailand. Many videos trace the origins of individual dishes, such as Pad Thai, Pad Ga Prau or Sriracha sauce. Other episodes cover old restaurants in Bangkok or the cuisine of lesser-known towns such as Phetchaburi. OTR stands for “On the Road and Off the Rails”.
