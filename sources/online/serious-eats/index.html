Serious Eats

American food site with many Thai recipes

Sources

“Serious Eats” is an American food site that tests its recipes thoroughly and explains the technique behind them. Many of its Thai recipes are by Leela Punyaratabandhu, who also writes the blog She Simmers. Others were developed by J. Kenji López-Alt, who led recipe development at Serious Eats for many years and wrote the cookbook “The Wok”. There are also recipes from cookbooks such as Pok Pok by Andy Ricker.
