Northern Thailand Sources

The Northern Information Center of Chiang Mai University (CMU) Library runs the “Lanna Food” database ( ). It documents traditional dishes from Northern Thailand with ingredients, preparation and tips. The recipes come from interviews with named experts from the region and are backed by references. For my encyclopedia, the database is an important source for dishes from Northern Thailand.
