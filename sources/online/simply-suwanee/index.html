Simply Suwanee

Thai recipes by Suwanee from Surin

Sources

The blog “Simply Suwanee” is run by Suwanee. She grew up in a village in Surin province in Isan and came to the United States at the age of 13. Cooking brought her back to her roots, and many of her recipes are shaped by the food of Isan. For my encyclopedia, the blog is an important source, especially for dishes from the Northeast.
